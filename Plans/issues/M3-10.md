## Problem

instructions for standing up an Android test environment. Yet the acceptance surface already
assumes it: M1-1 AC1 reads *"Camera" produces a stored image + Item draft row on a
simulator/emulator and iOS.* Without a documented Android test env, on-device/camera behavior on
Android is unverifiable and every device-touching AC can only be "waived." This item writes that
doc.

This is the **documentation half** of the Android pair; **M3-11** performs the *actual* setup on
this MacBook. They share a target; this issue produces prose + commands, M3-11 executes them.
